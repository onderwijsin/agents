# A-09 — Teaching opportunities with a certificate

## Bericht 1 — Adviesvrager

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to enquire about opportunities to work as an English teacher in
the Netherlands.

I am TEFL certified in [land buiten de EU] and have some experience teaching young
children, particularly [leeftijdsgroep]. I enjoy working with young learners
and am very interested in developing my teaching career in the Netherlands.

Could you please advise me on what qualifications or additional training I
would need to be eligible to teach children in the Netherlands? I would also
like to know whether your training centre provides any guidance or assistance
with finding teaching positions or placement opportunities after completing
the relevant training.

As I am currently based in [land buiten de EU], I would also appreciate any information you
can provide regarding the process for international applicants, including
whether you assist with employment opportunities or guidance on the
requirements for working in the Netherlands.

I would be grateful for any advice you can offer and would be happy to
provide my CV, TEFL certificate, and details of my teaching experience.

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,

## Bericht 2 — Adviseur

Dear [adviesvrager],

Thank you for your question to Onderwijsloket, I’m happy to think along!

Nice to hear that you want to work as a teacher in the Netherlands, and
already have an TEFL-certificate and some experience. To be able to work as a
teacher in the Netherlands, you need to have a teaching qualification or other
suitable certification, according to the rules of the Dutch government. Sadly,
a TEFL-certificate is not sufficient to teach at a Dutch school. Which
qualification or certification you need depends on the education sector in
which you want to teach. You can read all about the Dutch education
qualification system in [this
article](https://www.onderwijsloket.com/kennisbank/artikel-archief/teaching-qualifications-in-the-netherlands/).

You can obtain a teaching degree by following a fulltime or parttime teacher
training program at a university of applied sciences
([hogeschool](https://www.onderwijsloket.com/kennisbank/artikel-archief/how-is-the-dutch-education-system-structured/)). However, foreign students need to
show that their Dutch is at B2 level, before applying for a bachelor’s degree.
You can read more about the Dutch language requirements in this
[article](https://www.onderwijsloket.com/kennisbank/artikel-archief/do-i-have-to-speak-dutch-in-order-to-become-a-teacher-in-the-netherlands/). A notable
exception is the bachelor ‘Teaching Education in English’, obtainable at the
Hogeschool Utrecht. This is the only teacher training program in the
Netherlands that doesn’t require an understanding of Dutch at B2 level. You
can find more information on this program, including admission requirements,
on [their website](https://www.internationalhu.com/bachelor-programmes/teacher-education-in-english).

Furthermore, you could work at an international school. At this type of
schools, you don’t necessarily need a Dutch teaching qualification, but you
would still need a teaching degree of some sort to show your teaching
abilities. In this case, your TEFL-certificate and teaching experience might
be sufficient. International schools are private schools, so their
qualification requirements can differ from what is legally required in the
Netherlands. You can always contact a school to ask about their policy:
<https://www.dutchinternationalschools.nl/>.

I hope this provides an answer to your question. Feel free to contact us again
if you have any further questions!

Kind regards,
