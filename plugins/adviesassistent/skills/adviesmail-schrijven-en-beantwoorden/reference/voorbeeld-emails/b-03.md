# B-03 — Pathways into Dutch education

## Bericht 1 — Adviesvrager

Dear Onderwijsloket team,

I am writing to ask for advice about my opportunities to work in education in
the Netherlands in the future.

I hold a university degree in [degree subject], a Higher
Vocational Diploma in [degree subject], and a Master's degree in [degree subject]. I also have [number of] years of experience providing private tutoring in different subjects.

I will soon be moving to [country] for [period] to work as a
Spanish language teaching assistant at a secondary school. After completing
this experience, I would like to explore the possibility of returning to the
Netherlands and building a career in education.

I do not currently hold a formal teaching qualification, and I have not
completed any specific training in teaching Spanish as a foreign language.

I would be grateful for your advice on the following questions:

- Which teaching or educational support roles could I realistically apply for with my academic background and experience?
- Could I qualify as a secondary school teacher in Geography or another suitable subject through a teacher training programme or a work-based pathway such as _zij-instroom_?
- Are there opportunities to work in vocational education (MBO) while completing the required pedagogical training?
- Would it be possible to complete the necessary training in English, or would I need to reach a certain level of Dutch first?
- Are there any programmes that would allow me to earn a salary while training to become a qualified teacher?

I am particularly interested in teaching, coordinating groups and supporting
students' development. However, I am still exploring which educational role
would suit me best.

Thank you very much for your time and guidance. I would appreciate any
recommendations on the most realistic pathway for my situation.

Kind regards,

## Bericht 2 — Adviseur

Dear [adviesvrager],

Thanks for reaching out to het Onderwijsloket. I'm happy to think along.

I will try to answer your questions concisely below.

**Which teaching or educational support roles could I realistically apply for
with my academic background and experience?**  
As you do not hold a formal teaching degree and have not yet reached B2 level
of Dutch, I think it would be most realistic to pursue a role as a teacher in
higher education. Higher education comprises research universities
(universiteit) and universities of applied sciences (hogeschool). The
institutions themselves decide which requirements their teachers must meet;
there are no legal requirements. This means you could apply directly for a
teaching job. However, Dutch universities have drawn up a quality mark for
teachers. This so-called Basic Teaching Qualification (BKO) is proof of the
didactic competence of teachers at research universities and universities of
applied sciences. The training is typically offered by the university that
employs you. If you would like to read more, [this
article](https://onderwijsloket.com/kennisbank/artikelen/hoe-word-ik-docent-in-het-hoger-onderwijs) may be helpful (in Dutch, though).

**Could I qualify as a secondary school teacher in Geography or another
suitable subject through a teacher training programme or a work-based pathway
such as zij-instroom?**  
Yes, you may be able to obtain your teaching degree for geography through a
zij-instroomtraject. You would need to reach at least B2 level of Dutch,
though. An interesting option for you may be [this trajectory for
internationals](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/iclon/initial-teacher-education/programme-for-internationals), offered by Leiden University.

**Are there opportunities to work in vocational education (MBO) while
completing the required pedagogical training?**  
Yes, in theory there would be. You would also enter into a zij-
instroomtraject, specifically for mbo. This is called a pdg-traject. To my
knowledge, there are no pdg-trajectories in English, so you would have to
master Dutch at B2 level in order to follow the training. In [this
article](https://onderwijsloket.com/kennisbank/artikelen/wat-is-een-pdg-traject) you can read more about the trajectory (in Dutch).

**Would it be possible to complete the necessary training in English, or would
I need to reach a certain level of Dutch first?**  
To my knowledge, Hogeschool Utrecht is the only institution that offers
teacher training in English. They only offer these trajectories for language
teachers. You can read more about these programmes
[here](https://www.internationalhu.com/teacher-education-programmes). The
[programme for
internationals](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/iclon/initial-teacher-education/programme-for-internationals) I mentioned above could also be
interesting for you.

**Are there any programmes that would allow me to earn a salary while training
to become a qualified teacher?**  
In theory, there are opportunities that would allow you to earn a salary while
training. Yet most of these possibilities require you to master Dutch before
you enter.
