## Bericht 2 — Adviseur

Hi [adviesvrager],

Thank you for reaching out to Onderwijsloket, I’m happy to think along.

To teach at a Dutch primary school, you need a teaching certification for
primary schools. Your French teaching qualification needs to be recognized by
DUO, a governmental organization in charge of recognizing foreign teaching
qualifications. Furthermore, it is a requirement to have a Dutch language
level of B2 to teach at primary schools in the Netherlands. [In this
article](https://onderwijsloket.com/kennisbank/artikelen/how-can-i-become-a-teacher-in-the-netherlands-with-a-foreign-teaching-qualification) you can read
more about teaching in the Netherlands with a foreign teaching qualification.

In case you do not reach the Ducth B2 level, you could work at an
international school. At this type of schools, you don’t necessarily need a
Dutch teaching qualification, but you would still need a teaching degree of
some sort to show your teaching abilities. International schools are private
schools, so their qualification requirements can differ from what is legally
required in the Netherlands. You can always contact a school to ask about
their policy:
[_https://www.dutchinternationalschools.nl/_](https://www.dutchinternationalschools.nl/).
Another option is to teach at a French primary school located in the
Netherlands, which follows the curriculum and regulations of the French
education system.

If you would be interested to teach French as a language, the Hogeschool
Utrecht offers a bachelor’s program ‘[Teaching Education in
French](https://www.hu.nl/deeltijd-opleidingen/teacher-education-in-french)’.
This is the only teacher training program in the Netherlands that doesn’t
require an understanding of Dutch at B2 level. During the program, you will
also do Dutch courses, to prepare you to teach at Dutch schools.

Aside from that program, there are no specific Dutch Learning programs
designed for foreign teachers, but you can find NT2 courses for B2 level Dutch
at many universities and organizations in the Netherlands.

Hopefully this information has answered your questions. Feel free to contact
us again if you have any further questions!
